Recommendation: Option C (Minimal Redesign)

The minimal approach solves all issues while maintaining consistency:

  • White background with yellow accent border matches factor pages
  • Yellow CTA button with black text (9.5:1 contrast ratio)
  • Purple outline secondary button (4.82:1 contrast ratio)
  • Simple card hover states instead of glassmorphism
  • Removes broken legacy sections entirely

This creates a welcoming community page that's accessible, maintainable, and consistent with the overall site design.
